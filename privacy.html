This policy explains what information Holy Fitness collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language.

1. Who we are

Holy Fitness is run by [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us"). You can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Profile details you enter: age, gender, height, weight, estimated body fat, training experience, sleep, stress level, goal, diet style and food allergies or avoidances.
  • Health-check answers (sensitive): any conditions you tick, and for women, optional cycle phase, symptoms, PCOS, irregular periods or perimenopause. These are used to decide whether a plan can safely be built and to adjust it. We do not store your raw answers as a separate record, but the plan we save for you can include notes that refer to them in general terms (for example, "you mentioned joint problems").
  • Your plan and logs: your generated plan, which day you are on, and the workouts, meals and sleep you choose to log.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard server logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and time of request) for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other sites.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To build your personalised training, nutrition and sleep plan, and to keep it safe for you.
  • To save your plan and logs so they are available on all your devices.
  • To send you a password-reset email when you ask for one.
  • To protect the service against abuse (for example, limiting repeated failed sign-ins).

We handle your health-related answers on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create your account and complete the health check. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete your account. Password-reset links expire after one hour. When you delete your account, your account record, plan, progress and logs are removed from our database. Short-lived security records (such as a failed-sign-in counter) expire automatically within minutes.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: sign in, open Account, and choose Download my data.
  • Delete your data: in Account, choose Delete my account. This is permanent.
  • Correct your data: you can generate a new plan with updated details at any time, or contact us.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, your sign-in cookie cannot be read by page scripts, and repeated failed sign-ins are limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

Holy Fitness is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.
