This policy explains what personal information Holy Fitness collects, why, who handles it, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). We have tried to write it in plain language.

1. Who we are

The responsible party is Holy Fitness (“we”, “us”), based in South Africa [REGISTRATION NUMBER, if registered]. Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL], [PHYSICAL ADDRESS]. Our Information Officer is [INFORMATION OFFICER NAME].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Profile details you enter: age, gender, height, weight, estimated body fat, training experience, sleep, stress level, goal, diet style, and food allergies or avoidances.
  • Health-check answers (special personal information): any conditions you tick, and for women, optional cycle phase, symptoms, PCOS, irregular periods or perimenopause. POPIA treats health information as special personal information, so we process it only with your consent. We do not keep your raw answers as a separate record, but the plan we save for you can include notes that refer to them in general terms (for example, “you mentioned joint problems”).
  • Your body measurements: the weight, body-fat and waist figures you choose to log, the date of each, and any goal you set, so you can see your progress and update your plan.
  • Your plan, logs and history: your generated plan, which day you are on, the workouts, meals and sleep you choose to log, and a permanent day-by-day record of what you logged (including any notes and extra foods you typed in) so you can look back at past days, weeks and months.
  • Technical data: one session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard server logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP address and time of request) for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies and we do not track you across other websites.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To build your personalised training, nutrition and sleep plan, and to keep it safe for you.
  • To save your plan and logs so they are available on all your devices.
  • To send a password-reset email when you ask for one.
  • To protect the service against abuse (for example, limiting repeated failed sign-ins).

We only use your information for these purposes. You give us your consent to process your health-related information when you create your account and complete the health check. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account, though plans cannot then be provided.

4. Who handles it for us

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising. We use service providers (“operators” under POPIA) who process data on our behalf:

  • Vercel — hosts the website and application.
  • Upstash (through Vercel Storage) — the database that stores your account, plan and logs.
  • Resend [confirm the email provider you actually use] — sends password-reset emails.
  • Google Fonts — the website loads its typefaces from Google. When you open a page, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as with any website that uses Google Fonts.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

5. Storage outside South Africa

Our providers store and process data on servers outside South Africa (for example in the United States or Europe). By accepting this policy you consent to that transfer. We choose providers that apply security safeguards and are bound by their own data-protection commitments, as POPIA requires for cross-border transfers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete your account. Password-reset links expire after one hour. When you delete your account, your account record, plan, progress and logs are removed from our database. Short-lived security records (such as a failed-sign-in counter) expire automatically within minutes.

7. Your rights

  • See and download your information: sign in, open Account, and choose Download my data.
  • Delete it: in Account, choose Delete my account. This is permanent.
  • Correct it: generate a new plan with updated details at any time, or contact us.
  • Object, restrict or withdraw consent: contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will act on your request.
  • Complain: you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za if you believe we have not handled your information properly. We would appreciate the chance to fix the problem first.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, your sign-in cookie cannot be read by page scripts, and repeated failed sign-ins are limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If your personal information is accessed by an unauthorised person, we will notify the Information Regulator and you as POPIA requires.

9. Age

Holy Fitness is for adults aged 18 and over. POPIA treats anyone under 18 as a child, and processing a child’s information requires a parent or guardian’s consent, which this app is not set up to collect. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
