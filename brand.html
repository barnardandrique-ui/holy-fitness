Brand guidelines.

Holy Fitness is training with discipline and reverence: precise, calm and honest. The identity comes from the logo itself: a red H whose stem becomes a blade, a black F, and a golden halo.

The logo

The monogram is the primary mark. Use the full-colour version on light backgrounds and the reversed version (white F) on dark ones. The F is never black on a dark background.

Full colour · light backgrounds
Reversed · dark backgrounds
Clear spaceKeep a margin equal to the width of the F on every side. Nothing enters that space.
Minimum sizeNever smaller than 24 px high on screen, or 12 mm in print. Below that, use the favicon tile.
WordmarkHOLY FITNESS in Bodoni Moda Bold, all caps, letter-spaced 20%. Use the supplied lockups; don't retype it.
Don'tRecolour, stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, or place the logo on busy photos.

Colour

Three colours come straight from the logo. Everything else supports them. Red is for action and emphasis, so it stays rare. Gold is an accent, never a fill for large areas.
