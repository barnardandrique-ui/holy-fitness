The process

How Holy Fitness works.

01

Create Your Account

Sign up in seconds. Your profile secures your data and personalised plans.

02

Enter Your Stats

Gender, height, weight, body fat, age, training experience and lifestyle — all factored in.

03

Set Your Goal

Fat loss, muscle gain, recomposition, strength or performance. A quick health check keeps it safe, with optional cycle details.

04

We Build Your Plan

Your lean mass, calories and macros are calculated, then a unique 7-day roadmap is built: training split, full meals, and sleep protocol.

05

Execute & Regenerate

Follow your weekly plan, then regenerate for next week with progressive adjustments built in.
